Huiting Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 15, 2026
Applies to: Huiting for iOS (“the app”)
Contact: shengjianapp@163.com

In short

Recordings, transcripts and meeting notes are stored on your iPhone. The app has no accounts and uses no analytics or advertising tools. Only when you use “Generate minutes” is that meeting’s transcript forwarded by Huiting’s minutes service to the third-party AI service DeepSeek. Huiting’s minutes service forwards the text and does not store it.

1. Data stored on your iPhone

DataPurposeWhere it is stored
Audio you record or import in the appPlayback and transcriptionThe app’s storage on your iPhone
Transcripts, AI minutes and your bookmarksDisplay, search and exportSame as above
GlossaryHelping the minutes correct misheard wordsThe app’s settings
Install ID (randomly generated)Counting your free monthly minutesThe app’s settings

The developer cannot access this data (except the install ID, see section 3). Deleting a meeting deletes its audio and text; deleting the app deletes all data.

2. Speech-to-text happens on your iPhone

Transcription uses Apple’s built-in speech recognition and runs on your iPhone, so your audio does not leave the device for this. The first time, iOS may download a Chinese speech model from Apple; iOS handles that download.

The live text shown while recording is only for viewing and bookmarking at the time and is not saved. The final transcript is produced on the device after recording ends.

3. What leaves your iPhone when you generate minutes

Before the first time you generate minutes, the app explains the following and asks for your consent. After you agree it does not ask again, unless you turn on “Ask every time before generating minutes” in Settings.

What is sent

  • That meeting’s transcript and your glossary;
  • A randomly generated install ID, used to count free monthly minutes;
  • A device token generated by Apple’s DeviceCheck, used to confirm the request comes from Huiting on a real iPhone. It contains no personal information and the developer cannot use it to identify you.

What is not sent: the original audio, your other meetings, your bookmarks, your name or contact details.

Where it goes

  1. Huiting’s minutes service, operated by the developer and hosted on Cloudflare. It verifies the device token with Apple, forwards the transcript and glossary to DeepSeek, and sends the minutes back to your iPhone. It does not store transcripts or minutes. It only records how many minutes each install ID has used this month, deleted automatically after about 40 days. As the hosting provider, Cloudflare processes network information needed to deliver requests (such as IP addresses).
  2. DeepSeek produces the minutes under the developer’s DeepSeek account. You do not need to sign up or pay.

How DeepSeek handles the text

Once the text reaches DeepSeek, it is processed under DeepSeek’s own privacy policy and terms, which the app does not control. According to DeepSeek’s published privacy policy:

  • Data is processed and stored in the People’s Republic of China;
  • Submitted content may be used to train and improve its models;
  • The policy does not state a fixed retention period.

If a meeting involves confidential information, think carefully before generating minutes, or use only the on-device recording, transcription and export features. DeepSeek privacy policy: https://cdn.deepseek.com/policies/en-US/deepseek-privacy-policy.html

4. Export and sharing

When you export minutes, the app creates a Markdown file. You decide where to save it or whom to send it to through the iOS share or save sheet.

5. What the app does not do

  • No sign-up or login; there are no accounts.
  • No analytics, crash-reporting, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or renting of any data.

If you have turned on “Share with App Developers” in iOS settings, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer under Apple’s own policy.

6. Recording others

In many countries and regions, recording a conversation requires the other participants’ consent. Before recording with the app, inform participants and follow the laws where you are.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect children’s personal information.

8. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes materially, this page will be updated with a new effective date.
